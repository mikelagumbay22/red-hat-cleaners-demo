Bridal & South Asian Alterations

For Brides, Bridal Parties and Families

Your wedding outfit deserves a fit you'll feel good in all day. Our seamstress is experienced with both western and South Asian bridal wear, and customers trust her with the pieces that matter most.

  • Wedding gown alterations: fitting, hemming and adjustments so your gown fits the way it should.
  • Wedding gown cleaning: careful cleaning for your gown before or after the big day.
  • Lehengas: alterations for lehengas and other South Asian outfits.
  • Bridal blouses: fitting and adjustments for bridal blouses.
  • Indian suits: alterations for Indian suits for the wedding, the family and every celebration.
