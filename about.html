Our Shop

Red Hat Cleaners is a small, local dry cleaner and alterations shop at 735 Twain Avenue in Meadowvale Village, Mississauga. We look after everyday dry cleaning and tailoring, and we take special care with the outfits that matter most, like wedding gowns, lehengas and bridal blouses.

Skilled Hands You Can Trust

At the heart of the shop is a skilled, experienced seamstress trusted by longtime local customers. Many of them keep coming back, and they bring their families too.
